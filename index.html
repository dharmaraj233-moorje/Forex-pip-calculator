Trader's Professional Execution Rules

• For 5-digit Forex pairs (e.g. 1.08542), 1 pip = 0.0001 (the 4th decimal). For JPY pairs, 1 pip = 0.01 (2nd decimal).
• For Gold (XAUUSD), 1 standard lot is 100 troy ounces; a $0.10 price move equals 1 pip ($10/lot).
• For Silver (XAGUSD), 1 standard lot is 5,000 troy ounces; a $0.01 price move equals 1 pip ($50/lot).
• For Crude Oil (USOIL), 1 standard lot is 1,000 barrels. For Cryptos and Indices, pip values reflect index ticks and point spreads calibrated to account denomination.
